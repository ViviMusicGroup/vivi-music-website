More Than Just a Project

VIVI Music is a free and open-source music player for Android that brings together the best features from modern music apps. Built with premium design principles, it offers a beautiful, personalized experience that adapts to your style.

The project started as a passion project to create the ultimate music streaming experience, free from ads and subscriptions. Today, it's powered by a growing community of contributors who believe music should be accessible to everyone.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is VIVI Music? expand_more
VIVI Music is a free, fully open-source music player for Android. It streams high-fidelity music from YouTube Music completely ad-free and offers a premium, personalized user experience that adapts to your style.
Is VIVI Music free and ad-free? expand_more
Yes, VIVI Music is 100% free and open-source under the GPL-3.0 license. It does not contain any ads, tracking, telemetry, or hidden subscriptions.
Does it support offline playback? expand_more
Absolutely! You can download and cache your favorite tracks to listen offline. The app's storage management optimizes your local space automatically.
Is my data private? expand_more
Yes. VIVI Music values your privacy and has zero trackers, analytics, or telemetry. Your library, listening history, and custom settings are stored locally on your device.
How do I install or update VIVI Music? expand_more
You can download the APK file directly from our website using the download buttons or from our official GitHub Releases page. The app includes an in-built updater to seamlessly transition between Official and Nightly builds.
